# Chat

The athlete writes to the coach in the composer, sees the message and the coach's reply in the transcript, opens the slash-command list by typing `/`, and starts over with `New chat`.

## Sub-features

- `chat-greeting` shows `Hello, Ada.`, or `Hello.` after a skipped connect, above an empty transcript, with the disclaimer `Not medical advice, and not a substitute for a doctor or a certified coach.` under the composer.
- `chat-send` saves the athlete's message before the model runs, shows it in the transcript the moment Send is tapped, and clears the composer.
- `chat-draft` keeps typed, unsent text in the composer across a kill and relaunch, and shows `Not sent. Your draft is still here.` in `chat.composer.notSent` when the message could not be saved.
- `chat-reply` shows the coach's reply under the message.
- `chat-working` shows `Coach is working…` in `chat.working` under the message while the turn has no reply text yet.
- `chat-streaming` shows the reply growing under the message with the working row staying under the reply text while the coach is still answering.
- `chat-stop` shows `Stop responding` in `chat.stop` while a turn runs or waits; tapping it stops the turn and shows the streamed text dimmed with `This reply stopped before it finished. Nothing was changed.` and `Try again`. When information was saved before the stop, the notice reads `This reply stopped before it finished. Some information was saved first.` with no `Try again`. A message queued behind it, or still inside its join window, is stopped before it starts and shows `Received before the app closed. Tap Try again to send it.` with `Try again`. Records lists the running turn's `turnSettled` as `interrupted athleteStopped`.
- `chat-expiry` keeps a turn running after the phone locks under one execution lease, begun at Send or Try again and held until the chat has no work left. When iOS expires the lease, the running turn stops without a tap and shows the same interrupted notice with its streamed text, Records lists `interrupted systemExpired`, and queued turns show `Received before the app closed. Tap Try again to send it.` An expiry after coach memory was saved reads `This reply stopped before it finished. Some information was saved first.` with no `Try again`. Each `turnClaim` row names the lease kind iOS granted, `continuedProcessing` or `gracePeriodOnly`.
- `chat-finished-away` shows `Finished while the phone was locked.` in `chat.turn.finishedWhileLocked` under a reply that landed while the app was in the background, until the app relaunches. On a phone, the same reply posts one `Coach` notification with the start of the reply.
- `chat-failed` shows one catalog sentence in `chat.turn.notice` under the message when the model fails, chosen by the failure, with at most one action under it:
  - a server error, a network failure, a timeout, or the watchdog: `The model provider is having trouble — try again in a few minutes.` with `Try again` in `chat.turn.tryAgain`;
  - a rejected Credits key (401 or 403): `Your Credits couldn't be used. Restore purchases to continue.` with `Restore purchases` in `chat.turn.restorePurchases`, which opens Credits;
  - no Credits left (402): `You're out of Credits. Buy more, or switch to your OpenRouter account.` with `Buy Credits` in `chat.turn.buyCredits`, which opens Credits;
  - a rate limit: `Rate limited — please try again in ~7 seconds.` for a 7 second hint, `~2 minutes` for 90 seconds, or `about a minute` with no hint, with a `Try again` that stays disabled until the wait ends;
  - an overflow, a bad request, or a spent turn budget: `Sorry, something went wrong. Please try again.` with `Try again`;
  - a broken or unknown stream: `The coach couldn't respond. Please try again.` with `Try again`;
  - a locked keychain: `Unlock your iPhone to continue. Your message is saved.` with `Try again`;
  - no Credits key: `Choose how the coach reaches a model to continue.` with `Choose access method` in `chat.turn.chooseAccessMethod`, which opens the onboarding connect step until Settings exists.
  No status code, server body, or Swift type name is ever on screen. `chat.error` no longer exists for turns.
- `chat-retry` retries a failed attempt on its own before any notice appears, with npm's counts: a server error or network failure twice after short waits under 5 seconds, a rate limit three times after the hinted wait (or 5, 10, and 20 seconds), a timeout once, and an overflow three times after compacting. `chat.working` stays on screen through every wait with no countdown, and only the last failure shows a notice.
- `chat-saved-unverified` shows `I saved your information, but couldn't verify my response. Please try again.` in `chat.turn.notice` with no action when coach memory was saved before the reply failed. The sentence asks for a new message, not a replay. Records lists the `memorySection` row stamped with the turn's attempt. The next message's prompt carries this sentence as the coach's reply.
- `chat-try-again` answers the same message again under a new attempt; Records lists one more `turnClaim` and the turn's `turnSettled`.
- `chat-relaunch` reopens on the chat with the transcript after the app is killed and relaunched with the kept store.
- `chat-accepted-relaunch` shows a message that was sent but never started before the app closed once, with `Received before the app closed. Tap Try again to send it.` in `chat.turn.receivedBeforeClose` and `Try again`; nothing runs until the tap. A message queued behind a running reply when the app was killed reopens this way.
- `chat-interrupted-relaunch` shows a message whose reply had started when the app was killed with `This reply stopped before it finished. Nothing was changed.` in `chat.turn.notice` and `Try again`, never `Received before the app closed`. When coach memory was saved before the kill, the notice reads `This reply stopped before it finished. Some information was saved first.` with no `Try again`. No reply text shows, and relaunch starts no model call on its own. Records lists the turn's `turnSettled` row as `interrupted processEnded`.
- `chat-background` keeps a reply running when the app goes to the background and comes back; backgrounding alone never shows an interrupted notice. A message still inside its 1.5 second join window starts at once when the app goes to the background.
- `chat-memory-flush` saves what the coach learned. When the history before a message passes 80 percent of the prompt budget with at least five messages since the last saved batch, the coach writes a `flushPending` job before it answers, and after the reply it extracts memory into `memorySection` and `ledgerEvent` rows and writes `flushSettled`. A context overflow flushes the same way inside the turn before it compacts. A job that fails part way stays pending, and the next launch runs it again without starting a new one; a second run never duplicates a `ledgerEvent`. No flush shows on screen.
- `chat-summary` keeps a long conversation in the prompt. When the history no longer fits, the coach summarizes the oldest messages with the compaction model before it answers; an overflow compacts the same way. Records lists `compactionSummary` and `windowStart`. The next prompts start with `[Previous conversation summary]`, read in Debug as `fixture.historyHead`. The transcript still shows every message. When the summary call fails, the dropped messages stay in the prompt.
- `chat-coalesce` joins two free-text messages sent within 1.5 seconds into one turn whose athlete text has both lines; Records lists `userMessage 2` with one turn.
- `chat-review` answers `/review` with the Saturday group ride.
- `chat-slash-list` lists `/review`, `/status`, `/workout`, and `/language` above the composer, and never `/plan`.
- `chat-slash-fill` fills the composer with the chosen command and a space, which hides the list.
- `chat-plan` sends `/plan` to the coach as an ordinary message; the fixture answers with the week summary.
- `chat-new` clears the transcript back to the greeting.
- `chat-no-network` keeps `fixture.requestCount` at `0 requests` through every turn.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Finish onboarding. The chat opens.
- Tap `Message your coach`, type, and choose `Send message`.
- Type `/` as the first character in `Message your coach`.
- Choose `New chat` in the top bar.
- Choose `Menu`, then `Debug`, to read the request count.

## Gotchas

- The fixture transport answers with no delay unless the message is `fixture:slow`. Only that directive keeps `chat.working` and the streamed text on screen long enough to capture.
- A `fixture:` directive is typed as a message. `fixture:slow`, `fixture:hang`, and `fixture:fail ...` appear in the transcript as the athlete's message. `fixture:storage fail-next-append` arms the record store before its own save, so it is the message that fails and stays in the composer.
- The soft flush and the trim follow the real prompt budget of about 28,500 tokens, so the short fixture replies never reach them. `fixture:long` answers with about 5,200 tokens: five of them put the next message past the soft gate, and six put the next message past the budget. A transcript of long replies makes XCUITest queries slow and flaky on the simulator, so drive those states in hosted tests and reach flush and summary states in proofs through `fixture:fail overflow`. A flush that runs after a reply keeps `chat.working` off screen.
- `fixture:flush-partial` arms the next memory flush, whichever message triggers it: its first step saves a `schedule` section and a `ledgerEvent`, then that run and the next drain of the job fail, so the job stays pending until the next launch. After a relaunch the fixture transport has no flush script, and the drained job settles as nothing to save.
- `fixture:fail overflow` flushes memory inside the turn before it compacts, so `fixture:fail overflow x4` makes eight model requests: four replies, one flush, and three compactions.
- `fixture:fail <kind>` fails one model call. Append `xN`, as in `fixture:fail network x3`, to fail N calls in a row. The coach retries retryable failures on its own, so a single `fixture:fail 500`, `network`, `timeout`, `overflow`, or `429` ends in the week summary, not a notice. Use `x3` for server and network errors, `x2` for timeouts, and `x4` for rate limits and overflows to see the notice.
- Fixture waits are real. The fixture date stays fixed, but a retry waits the real seconds: `fixture:fail 429 7 x4` takes about 21 seconds before the notice.
- `fixture:slow` and `fixture:hang` apply to that message only. The next plain message answers at once. `Try again` on a directive message replays only the scripted reply, never the directive, so a retried `fixture:hang` answers with the week summary.
- Every send waits 1.5 seconds for a second message to join the same turn before the coach starts. Replies arrive that much later than the tap.
- After `fixture:hang`, wait the full 60 seconds. Sending another message before the watchdog fires queues it behind the hung turn.
- The fixture's execution host is `ImmediateExecutionHost`. It grants continued processing to every Send and Try again, keeps no real background task, and expires only through `fixture:expire` or `-EnduragentFixtureHost "expire-after N"`. A simulator run cannot prove the iOS progress interface, the notification, or a cancel from the system interface; the device probe does.
- `fixture:expire` is also a message. It expires the leases before its own send, so it runs afterwards as a new turn and gets the week summary.
- `-EnduragentFixtureHost "expire-after N"` expires every lease N seconds after it begins, so a later Send starts a new lease that expires again.
- To queue a message behind a running reply, start with `fixture:hang` and wait for `turnClaim 1` before sending the next message. A plain message sent inside the first message's 1.5 second window resets `fixture:slow` before its request starts, so the first reply then arrives at once.
- `app.terminate()` in a proof and `sim.mjs launch --keep` kill the app without `willTerminate`, so a reply cut off that way reopens as `interrupted processEnded` with no partial text. Only a real `willTerminate` keeps the partial text and records `interrupted appTerminating`; the hosted test `willTerminateSettlesTheRunningTurnBeforeItReturns` posts that notification.
- A kill within 1.5 seconds of Send lands before the reply starts, so the message reopens as `Received before the app closed` instead of the interrupted notice. Wait for `turnClaim 1` in Records before the kill.
- The fixture picks a reply from the message text, and any unmatched text gets the week summary. A wrong prompt still gets a reply, so assert the specific reply text.
- `/status` and `/workout` get the week summary in the fixture, and the command itself appears as the athlete's message. The workout preview needs a message containing `endurance ride`.
- `TutorialHarness.waitForLabel` waits 10 seconds for an exact label before it falls back to a `CONTAINS` match. Replies longer than the expected fragment still pass, but each such wait adds 10 seconds.
- The fixture calendar is fixed on 1998-06-15, so a rate limit's wait has always ended by the time the notice shows, and its `Try again` is enabled at once. A fixture run cannot show the disabled wait.
- `-EnduragentFixtureKeychain empty` only skips installing the Credits key, so use it with a fresh store. A kept store still holds the key an earlier launch installed.
- `Choose access method` opens the onboarding connect step, and `Start chatting` from there opens a new chat until the app keeps one conversation.
- `New chat` has no accessibility identifier. Find it by its label.
- The menu sheet has no close button. Swipe down to dismiss it, twice from the Debug screen.
- Typing `/` into a composer that already has text does not open the list. The list shows only while the composer starts with `/` and has no space.
