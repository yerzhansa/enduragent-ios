# Workout review

The coach prepares calendar changes during a turn, and the app commits them as one Workout review when the reply ends. The card appears after the turn that committed it, or at the top of a new conversation marked Prepared earlier, and never during the reply. The athlete approves or cancels the review as one decision. While a decision runs, the bar's buttons are disabled and a second tap is dropped; the hosted `FixtureReviewStageTests.aSecondTapWhileTheFirstDecisionRunsIsDroppedAndLeavesNoExpiredNotice` proves it. A finished review leaves a note box in the transcript: `chat.note` with one line per change, then its closing line in `chat.note.closing`.

## Sub-features

- `preview-show` displays the workout cards after the turn's reply: the review's title in its own transcript row, then one row per card. A review that arrives, or that is open when the app relaunches, opens with its title and first card on screen, never at its last card. The buttons sit in the review bar docked above the composer; at accessibility text sizes the bar instead closes the review in the transcript, after the kept workouts and the notice, and the health disclaimer, which has no identifier, moves from under the composer to the last row of the transcript, after the bar, so each bar line and control fits the visible transcript with the keyboard down. The review opens at its title once the title row is laid out; no other transcript row can start or use up that scroll. They come from the control set and wait for the review's token, which the app asks for only after every card of the revision has been on screen once, so no button exists before the last card has been on screen. A card counts as on screen when at least half of it, or half of the visible transcript for a card taller than that, sits between the navigation bar and the composer or docked bar after the layout settles; a card laid out by a scroll or a layout pass that is replaced before it is drawn, or one behind the composer, does not count.
- `preview-control-sets` shows one layout per control set: Cancel and an approve button named for the changes (Add to calendar for additions only, Update calendar for edits only, Remove from calendar for deletions only, Apply changes for a mix); Try again and Cancel after a change that was not saved; Check again and Cancel for an unconfirmed change; Cancel alone for a blocked review. A finished review has no button.
- `preview-add` runs the review and saves one outcome line per change in `chat.note`, such as `Endurance with tempo, 6/16/1998: change saved.` A change whose answer stayed unconfirmed reads `change may have been saved. Check your calendar.` The lines survive relaunch and are readable in History, as `archive.note`, after New conversation.
- `preview-cancel` closes the review without writing the remaining changes. It stays closed after another message and after relaunch.
- `preview-lost-answer` reads the calendar at once when a write loses its answer. A change found there gives `change seen on the calendar.` with no second write.
- `preview-unconfirmed` keeps an unconfirmed change with Check again and Cancel while the calendar cannot be read. Each time the app comes to the front it reads the change again. A change read as absent goes back to the remaining work with the `workouts.stopped` notice, Cancel and Add to calendar.
- `preview-account-changed` blocks a review prepared for another athlete when Add is tapped. The sentence shows once as `chat.review.notice` inside the card, also after a relaunch, and only Cancel remains. A review whose intervals.icu connection was removed instead reads `intervals.icu is not connected. Connect to add workouts to your calendar.` with Connect in `chat.review.connect`; see the [Settings page](./settings.md).
- `preview-cannot-verify` shows `Couldn't check your intervals.icu connection, so nothing was changed. Try again in a moment.` as `chat.review.notice` when the check before a decision cannot read the connection or calendar, with Cancel and the approve button again.
- `preview-upgrade` opens reviews saved by build 6833d90f. An open review closes with `This review was prepared before the app was updated. Ask the coach again.` and no control, offline and online. An unknown write opens as an unconfirmed change offline. Online, the read finds nothing and the card offers Cancel and Add to calendar again. A completed review keeps its notes.
- `preview-storage-unavailable` shows `review.storageUnavailable` on the card when the saved review cannot be read, with no enabled control. The card is readable again the next time the app comes to the front.
- `review-cards-add` shows one card per change. An add card reads its title, then `Add · 1 of 4 · 6/16/1998 · 50 min`, a chart drawn from its steps, the steps as readable lines and `Estimated training load: 42`. A strength card shows its effort and description instead of a chart.
- `review-cards-edit` shows an edit with its current and proposed values, such as `Duration: 60 min → 75 min`, `Estimated training load: 40 → 60` and `The workout steps change.`. After the coach renames it, the card adds `Name: Endurance → Threshold 3x10` and keeps its chart and steps. A rename or a date move alone is one line.
- `review-cards-delete` names the workout, says `This workout will be removed from the calendar.` and shows no load. A delete-only review offers Remove from calendar and no Add to calendar. After approval a finished delete moves under `Already completed`; a delete card still on the review reads `Not removed` or `May have been removed`.
- `review-totals` shows one totals line in the bar, such as `2 additions, 1 edit, 1 deletion · 155 min for added or edited workouts`. Minutes count only adds and edits, and the line reads `total duration unknown` when any of them has no known duration. Under the last card, `Existing workouts kept` lists each affected date's kept workouts, such as `Commute · 45 min` under `6/17/1998`.
- `review-approval-bar` asks one question above the buttons: `Add this workout to your calendar?`, `Update this workout on your calendar?`, `Remove this workout from your calendar?`, the plural of each for several cards, or `Apply all reviewed changes?` for a mix. Until every card has been on screen it reads `Scroll through every card to continue.` and shows no button.
- `review-card-failed` handles a card that could not be shown. The bar reads `Card 40 of 40 could not be shown. Nothing was changed.` with Show again and no approve button. Show again scrolls back to the title, rebuilds every card, and the controls return only after each card has been on screen again.
- `review-partial` shows a run that stopped at a refused change. The finished changes sit apart under `Already completed`, one line each, such as `Endurance 50, 6/16/1998: change saved.`; the remaining changes keep their full cards, the refused one marked `Not saved`. After the kept workouts the card reads `intervals.icu refused a change, so the remaining changes were stopped. Changes already made are kept. Check the rest before you try again.` once, with Try again and Cancel.
- `review-retried` finishes the remaining changes after Try again. The transcript keeps one note box with a line per change and the closing line `All reviewed changes are complete.`; a delete the calendar no longer had reads `no longer on the calendar.`, not `change saved.`.
- `review-sending` shows `Making the reviewed changes on your intervals.icu calendar…` while a change is being written, the finished changes under `Already completed`, the change in flight marked `Sending…`, and no button.
- `review-unconfirmed` shows a change whose answer was lost while the calendar could not be read. The card is marked `May have been saved` and reads `A change may have been saved to your intervals.icu calendar. Check again to find out before anything else is changed.` with Check again and Cancel. After Check again finds it, the line moves under `Already completed` as `change seen on the calendar.` with the `workouts.stopped` notice, Try again and Cancel.
- `review-refreshed` shows a review whose edited workout changed on the calendar after it was reviewed. The card reads `Workouts in this review changed in intervals.icu after you reviewed them, so nothing was changed. Check them again before you approve.` and `Endurance: duration changed from 60 to 90 minutes.`, and asks for approval again with Cancel and Apply changes.
- `review-blocked` shows a review that cannot continue with its reason on the card, before and after a relaunch, and Cancel alone. A missing workout reads `A workout in this review is no longer on your intervals.icu calendar, so this review can't continue. Cancel it and ask the coach again.`; a past workout, one the coach did not add, an entry that is not a workout, and a workout reviewed before the app was updated, `This workout was reviewed before the app was updated. Ask the coach again.`, each have their own sentence.
- `review-canceled` closes the review after Cancel. The note keeps the finished lines, gives `change not made.` for the rest and closes with `The remaining changes were canceled.`. A review canceled before anything was written gives `change not made.` for every change.
- `review-completed` closes with the note box and `All reviewed changes are complete.`. A change found on the calendar after a lost answer reads `change seen on the calendar.` and the others `change saved.`.
- `review-storage` shows only `review.storageUnavailable` as `chat.preview.notice` when the run cannot record a step, with no button and no stage notice.
- `review-not-prepared` shows `The workout review could not be prepared.` as `chat.turn.reviewNotice` under a turn whose preparation was refused, with no review card. When that turn saved information first, the line reads `Your information was saved, but the workout review could not be prepared.`
- `review-notes-language` reads `Prepared earlier` and the outcome note with its closing line in German, Portuguese of Portugal and Traditional Chinese at the largest accessibility text size.
- `preview-composer` keeps review rows above the opaque composer when the keyboard opens for a plain message. Send stays hittable.
- `review-long` keeps a review of any size whole and reachable, with no limit on the number of changes. `fixture:review-eight` prepares eight workouts over ten days, four rides and four strength sessions; `fixture:review-twenty` and `fixture:review-forty` prepare 20 and 40 strength sessions. Scrolling from the title reaches every card in order, `Add · 1 of 8 · 6/16/1998 · 50 min` to the last, then `Existing workouts kept` with Commute, Endurance and Recovery spin under 6/17, 6/18 and 6/19, then the bar with the totals, such as `8 additions, 0 edits, 0 deletions · 320 min for added or edited workouts`, and exactly one approve button.
- `review-largest-text` keeps the review readable and every control set tappable at the largest accessibility text size in all 17 languages. At these sizes the bar is the last part of the review in the transcript, not docked, so its text wraps instead of being clipped and the transcript keeps its reading area. On the 40-change review, Add to calendar and Cancel are each brought whole into the visible transcript, hittable, enabled and at least 44 points tall, and Cancel closes the review. The chart sizes its axis gutter and height from its labels, so at the largest size its labels neither overlap nor leave the card.
- `review-voiceover` reads the review in one order at the default and the largest text size: the review title, each card's lines from its title in card order, the kept workouts, the notice, then the bar's totals, question and controls. VoiceOver reads the chart's axis labels and skips its drawing, because the step lines say the same. XCUITest cannot drive VoiceOver itself; the accessibility snapshot order and the frames are what VoiceOver reads from.
- `review-language` shows every review string in the display language: the title, card headings, change lines, Load, step headings such as Warmup, Main set and Cooldown, the kept workouts, totals, question, approve and Cancel buttons, `workouts.bar.unseen`, `workouts.bar.cardFailed`, Show again, the outcome lines, every stage notice (sending, refused, unconfirmed, stopped, refreshed, blocked and storage), `Already completed` with its lines, the card statuses `Not saved`, `May have been saved` and `Sending…`, the outcome note's closing line after Try again and after Cancel, and `workouts.notPrepared` under a turn. In German, Portuguese of Portugal and Traditional Chinese no review string falls back to English, no placeholder is left unresolved, and the accessibility audit finds no clipped text on the review or its controls.
## How to get to it (user POV)

- Send `Give me a 60 minute endurance ride for tomorrow with two 10 minute tempo blocks` from the conversation.
- Choose Cancel or Add to calendar on the resulting review.
- Send `fixture:review-eight`, `fixture:review-twenty` or `fixture:review-forty` from a connected conversation for a long review, and scroll through every card to reach the approve button.
- Change the connected athlete through Settings > intervals.icu, `settings.training`, while a review exists, or through the peer controls in Settings > Debug.
- Launch with `FixtureArguments(reviewStage:)` and send `fixture:review-mixed`, then tap Apply changes. `.partial` refuses the edit once and answers each delete as gone, `.sending` holds the second write, `.unconfirmed` loses the first answer and fails the read after it, `.refreshed` lengthens workout 301 to 90 minutes after the review read it, `.blocked` removes workout 302 after the review read it, and `.storage` fails the review record that saves the first answer.
- Send `fixture:review-not-prepared` for a turn that prepares two reviews at once, so both are refused.
- Launch a build 6833d90f store with `FixtureArguments(store: .openReviewBeforeUpgrade, onboarded: true)`, `.unknownWriteBeforeUpgrade` or `.completedBeforeUpgrade`. Add `calendarReadFault: .failOnce` to open it offline.

## Largest text size and VoiceOver order

Each flow launches in English at the default text size, finishes onboarding or opens the upgrade store, chooses the language in Settings > Language and reaches its first state. It then relaunches the same store with `FixtureArguments.textSize = .accessibilityXXXL`, which passes `-UIPreferredContentSizeCategoryName UICTContentSizeCategoryAccessibilityXXXL`. Only the review is driven at the largest size, so typing and Settings stay at the size the harness is proven at. The unconfirmed flow keeps `calendarReadFault: .failOnce` on the relaunch, so the change is still unconfirmed when it is read at the largest size.

For each state the proof reads accessibility snapshots of the review's transcript rows, without the bar, and one of the review bar. The rows must read, in order, the localized title, the card lines starting with the card's title, any kept workouts, then the notice lines. The bar must be inside the transcript and start below the review's last line, and it must read the totals line, the question when Add to calendar is offered, then each control with its catalog title. Each element must start below or to the right of the one before it and stay inside its row or bar. Each control must be enabled and at least 44 points tall, which also fails if the text size did not apply. `performAccessibilityAudit` then checks clipped text, Dynamic Type and hit regions for the elements inside the row and the bar, page by page down the transcript, and the disclaimer must end the transcript. XCUITest cannot drive VoiceOver itself; the snapshot order and the frames are what VoiceOver reads from. Screenshots are named `u9-2-<tag>-<state>`.

The proof does not open Settings > Debug at the largest text size, so it does not read the fixture request count.

## Gotchas

- `fixture:review-mixed`, `fixture:review-edit`, `fixture:review-delete`, `fixture:review-eight`, `fixture:review-twenty` and `fixture:review-forty` seed three calendar workouts (301 Endurance on 6/18, 302 Recovery spin on 6/19, 303 Commute on 6/17, not made by the coach) and prepare their review. `fixture:review-eight` prepares rides `Ride 1`, `Ride 3`, `Ride 5` and `Ride 7` and strength sessions `Strength 2`, `Strength 4`, `Strength 6` and `Strength 8` on 6/16, 6/17, 6/18, 6/19, 6/21, 6/22, 6/24 and 6/25. `fixture:review-twenty` and `fixture:review-forty` prepare 20 and 40 strength sessions, one a day from 6/16. `fixture:review-rename` revises a waiting review so the edit is named `Threshold 3x10`. All of them need a connected athlete.
- `FixtureArguments(presentationFault: .failLastCardOnce)` makes the last card of a review fail to present the first time it is on screen. Show again presents it whole.
- The fixture presents a review for a `fixture:review-*` directive, or for a message that contains `endurance ride` or `60 minute endurance` and does not start with `/workout` or `/status`, and only with a connected athlete; an unconnected chat gets the missing-training-data reply. `/workout`, alone or with text after it, waits 8 seconds and replies in text with no workout review.
- The card is committed when the reply ends. Wait for the turn to settle before you look for it.
- `fixture.failNextAppend` fails the next record append. Arm it through `TutorialHarness.fixtureControl` in Settings > Debug after the review is presented and the proposing turn has settled, then tap a button. Reach every Debug row through `TutorialHarness.debugRow`, and use `TutorialHarness.returnToChat` to leave Settings or the language picker.
- `FixtureArguments(calendarSaveFault: .loseAnswerOnce)` stores the change before losing the answer, so the immediate read finds it. `calendarReadFault: .failOnce` affects only the next event list or event fetch; at launch that is the read of an unconfirmed change. The fake calendar starts empty at every launch, so an upgraded unknown write is read as absent once reads work.
- The build 6833d90f stores belong to athlete `i1001` under a fixed connection that the store policy installs. The fixture clock stays on 1998-06-15.
- The fake intervals.icu client keeps calendar writes in memory. The outcome lines and Records are the visible evidence because the app has no calendar screen.
- A waiting review has no timer. It survives New conversation and shows once at the top of the new one.
- The compact phone can truncate a long workout description. Inspect the visible cards and controls, and preserve the screenshot rather than assuming every step fits.
- Interactive captures must restore light appearance afterwards.
- The keyboard proof requires the software keyboard. Also inspect the opaque composer after manually scrolling rows under it.
